About the Company

Over 17yrs Industry Experience

A.B.U. Fire Protection is a professional fire protection and life safety company. We provide complete fire protection, sprinkler, life safety, and security services for residential and commercial properties across Ontario. All of our products are ULC approved, so you can count on your property meeting the highest Canadian standards for safety and performance.

With over 17 years of industry experience, our team brings extensive technical knowledge in fire alarm installation, inspections, testing, programming, troubleshooting, maintenance, repairs and system upgrades. We work with leading manufacturers, including Simplex, Mircom and Notifier.
